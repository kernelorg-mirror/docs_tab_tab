Minutes - Aug. 1st, 2019
========================

**Attendees:** Ted, Greg, Jon, Laura, Steven, Dan, Chris, Tim, Kees, Olof

Minutes review
--------------

The minutes from the June meeting were approved.
 
Code of Conduct
---------------

The Code of Conduct committee has prepared its first report, and will publish it to the community shortly.  The TAB had made several recommendations via e-mail discussions.  We can adjust the format of the report based on feedback received from the community.  Kristen Accardi accepted a position on the committee right before a sabbatical, but she will be returning shortly.  Greg will update the official Code of Conduct kernel.org web site accordingly.

TAB election
------------

The TAB is preparing for the elections, using the new online voting system, coming up in Plumbers in September.  There will be a face-to-face meeting, but voting will be done via an online process.  It was decided to end the voting before the end of the conference, in order to give time for recovery in case of some problem, and to allow for announcement of the newly elected members at the event.  Laura will prepare a nomination announcement to send out on community mailing lists.

Plumbers
--------
Arrangement for Plumbers 2019 are going well.  The only negatives are that the hotel is sold out, and the event is sold out, and we have a large wait list for the event.  The TAB discussed how to select people from the wait list, as well as some ideas for handling the large interest in the event.  Venues and pricing for future plumbers events in North America were discussed.
